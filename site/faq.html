FAQ

Common Queries

Is the WeBand waterproof?

It is made of a special waterproof silicone compound that is resistant to high and low temperatures. Even bathing or swimming can be worn. Avoid contact with chemical components Shower Gel.

Will the negative ions in the product last for indefinite period?

The negative ions emitted by the product have a certain time effect. After being exposed to skin heat and air, the negative ions content will not change much within 3 years under normal wear and maintenance, and the negative ions content will gradually decrease with use after 3 years.

Is the WeBand suitable for everyone to wear?

Pregnant women, patients with severe heart disease, and those who use medical electronic devices embedded in the body such as cardiac pacemakers, and patients with severe cardiovascular and cerebrovascular diseases, is recommended to consult a doctor before using our product.

How do I maintain the WeBand for daily usage? Can the product be cleaned?

You can use clean water for cleaning. Let dry naturally. If it stick to dust during wearing, use tape or stickers to remove the dust.

Can I wear the WeBand while sleeping?

It is better to wear it while sleeping. Helps sleep quality.

Are there any side effects to wearing the WeBand?

The main raw materials of negative ion energy products are made of safe food appliance grade silicone, similar to baby pacifiers or human body silicone supplement materials, so please feel free to wear it.

What shall I do for first time use?

Please wash with water a few times and discard the water. (For external usage, do not need to wash, use directly. Best to keep it dry)

How long can the WeTube negative ions last?

WETUBE energy function can last for 2 years as a water purifier and 4 years as a wearable product. It will depend on the frequency of usage, water conditions, and maintenance of the WETUBE.

How do I maintain the WeTube for daily usage? Can it be cleaned with any detergent?

You can just use clean water for cleaning and let it dry naturally. Do not use any detergent to wash it.

Are there any side effects on the usage of WeTube?

The main raw materials of negative ion energy products are from natural earth materials like Ion energy stone, Tourmaline, Maifan, Volcanic, negative Ion stone, Far-infrared stone which are non-toxic. The tube is made of Porcelain is a material that is strong, smooth, and non-toxic but breakable. It has a high level of mechanical resistance, low porosity, and high density, which, on a daily basis, provide it with durability, innocuity, soft touch and beauty.

How can I prevent my WeTube from break?

Store your WETUBE in a safe place, away from young kids which may cause it to accidentally fall to the floor. WETUBE will not break easily in hot water and hot sun.
