Our Story

It started with family

Greatest Gift of Health.

Wellnergie began with our founder, Mr Thomas Tan, and his desire to help his parents.

For many years, he witnessed his parents experience long term health challenges. Seeing the people he loved struggle with their wellbeing inspired him to search for another way to support a healthier and more comfortable life.

That personal search became the beginning of Wellnergie.

From a Personal Wish to a Greater Mission

Working alongside technical and research teams, our founder began exploring the relationship between energy, natural materials and the human body.

Through years of research, experimentation and refinement, we developed our approach to natural therapy, bringing together carefully selected natural materials and energy based technology to create innovative wellness solutions for everyday life.

Our Mission

To bring innovative wellness technology to people around the world, thoughtfully designed to support a healthier, more balanced and elevated way of living.

Our Vision — Building a Healthier Future Together

To pursue excellence in quality, advance wellness technology and build Wellnergie into a trusted international wellness brand.

Why Wellnergie

Wellness + Energy

The name Wellnergie combines the two ideas at the heart of our brand.

Wellness represents our purpose and our desire to help people live healthier, more energetic and fulfilling lives.

Energy represents our technological direction and our exploration of energy based applications using natural materials.

Together, they form Wellnergie.

Our WE logo represents the first letters of Wellness and Energy. The rising sun above the letters symbolises light, vitality, renewal and positive energy reaching people around the world.

It reflects our belief that health brings light into life.

Our Philosophy

Health is not just number one. It is the only one.

Modern life can place significant demands on our bodies. Stress, irregular routines, poor sleep, sedentary lifestyles and demanding schedules can all affect how we feel each day.

Sometimes these effects may be mild and easy to overlook, but they can still influence our comfort, energy and quality of life. We believe this is why everyday wellness matters.

Taking care of ourselves should not begin only when something goes wrong. It should be part of how we live every day.

Our Values

Health first. People always.

Wellnergie was created from a desire to help the people we love.

That spirit remains at the heart of our company. We believe business should create value for people and contribute positively to society.

Since our beginning in Singapore, we have continued to develop, learn and grow.

As more people discover our products and place their trust in Wellnergie, we remain committed to the same purpose that started it all: helping people live healthier, more energetic lives.
